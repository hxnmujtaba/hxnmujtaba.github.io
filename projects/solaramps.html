SolarAMPs
ERP Platform

SolarAMPs is an enterprise resource planning platform targeting solar industry specifically.

Role Senior Product Designer
Domain Simplified Complex Workflows
Platform Faster Execution

SolarAMPs is an enterprise resource planning platform targeting solar industry specifically. It consists of multiple modules; some of them are:  

• Sales Pipeline Management
• Project Management
• Garden Management
• Billing & Invoicing Management
• Work Order Management
• Marketplace
• Timesheet Management
• Widget Management

My Responsibilities

while addressing the needs of diverse personas including Enterprise POCs, employees, field workers, and customers.

My responsibilities included:

• Brainstorm with Business Analysts & Product Managers
• Define user personas and flows
• Create wireframes and full product designs
• Collaborate in project team meetings
• Conduct usability testing
• Handoff designs to developers

UX Challenges

The project presented several UX challenges:

• Ensuring Cohesive Navigation across multiple modules
• Designing Data-Dense dashboards that present complex information clearly
• Integrating Workflow Automation for Notifications, Approvals, and Task Assignments
while addressing the needs of diverse personas including Enterprise POCs, employees, field workers, and customers.

AI-Assisted Design & Intelligence

I leveraged AI-assisted design workflows to accelerate delivery and improve decision-making across the product lifecycle:

• AI-assisted wireframing and layout exploration for rapid UI ideation
• AI-supported usability testing analysis to extract insights efficiently
• AI-generated design documentation and assets for faster developer handoff

Additionally, the platform incorporated AI-powered features for end users, such as predictive sales insights and analytics widgets to help managers identify bottlenecks.

Impact

• Increased visibility into project status, billing, and service requests → faster decision-making for managers
• Reduced design iteration time by 25% using AI-assisted workflows
• Delivered the full product design independently, accelerating the project timeline by 20%
